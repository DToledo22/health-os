    Quick wins

      Watch closely

      Effect sizes are differences in your own average recovery between days with and without each factor. "Strong" means the gap is unlikely to be noise (t ≥ 2.5); "moderate" t ≥ 1.7. Patterns, not diagnoses.

      Health Monitor

      Each morning's vitals against your own prior-30-day typical range

      Daily value · shaded = your typical range at the time · dashed = 30-day median

      Recent flags (last 30 days)

      Typical range = 5th–95th percentile of your previous 30 mornings. Watch = past that range in the unhealthy direction; Alert = more than 2.5 standard deviations out, or three or more vitals off at once (the illness signature). Not a diagnosis — a prompt to pay attention.
